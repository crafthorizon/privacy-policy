This Privacy Policy explains what information the StormPing: Weather Alerts mobile application ("StormPing", "the app", "we", "our") uses, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android app published on Google Play by Craft Horizon (package: com.crafthorizon.stormping).

StormPing shows weather forecasts for places around the world, and severe weather alerts where a public alert service is available (the United States, Canada, Europe, the Philippines and Indonesia).

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not install or use the app.

1Summary

  • No account or registration is required.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or payment information.
  • Your location is used only if you allow it, only while you use the app, and only to show the weather where you are. StormPing never asks for background location access.
  • To get forecasts and alerts, the app sends the coordinates of your places (or the text you type in search) to public weather services. It sends nothing that identifies you.
  • Your saved locations and settings are stored only on your phone. We have no server and do not receive your data.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google and its mediation partners collect device identifiers and related data to show and measure ads. The app never gives them your saved locations or GPS position.
  • The app contains no analytics SDK, no Firebase and no in-app purchases.
  • We do not sell your data.

2Location

When you tap "Use my location", the app asks Android for permission to read your location (approximate or precise, your choice). If you allow it:

  • The location is read only while the app is open and in use. The app never requests background location access.
  • The last location found is saved on your phone as your "current location", so the app can show it again and, if you turn notifications on, check it for new alerts.
  • Outside the United States, your phone's built-in geocoding service (provided by your device, usually Google) turns the coordinates into a place name.

You can refuse or turn off location access at any time in Android Settings > Apps > StormPing > Permissions. The app keeps working with city search.

3Information Stored Only on Your Device

The app saves the following data locally on your phone so it works between sessions: up to 5 saved places and the last "current location", your settings (temperature unit, theme, notification choices and severity level), recently downloaded forecasts and alerts (a short cache so the app opens quickly), the list of alerts you were already notified about (so you are not notified twice), Ad-Free Break time, and simple counters used to space out ads fairly and to ask for a rating only once.

This data is never uploaded to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it permanently. You can also remove any saved place in the Locations screen.

4Weather Services the App Contacts

To show forecasts and alerts, the app connects directly from your phone to these public services. They receive the coordinates of the place and your IP address, as any website you visit does, but no name, account or advertising ID:

  • United States: National Weather Service API (api.weather.gov), a U.S. government service, for forecasts and alerts
  • Canada: Environment and Climate Change Canada (api.weather.gc.ca) for alerts
  • Everywhere: Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) for forecasts outside the United States and for day details (sunrise, UV index, humidity) for every place
  • City search: the text you type (a city name or ZIP code) is sent to Open-Meteo's place search (geocoding-api.open-meteo.com)

For alerts in Europe, the Philippines and Indonesia, the app downloads each country's public list of alerts (MeteoAlarm feeds.meteoalarm.org, PAGASA publicalert.pagasa.dost.gov.ph, BMKG bmkg.go.id) and checks on your phone which ones cover your place, so your coordinates are not sent to those services.

These services are run by third parties under their own policies. Weather data from Open-Meteo is licensed under CC BY 4.0 (credit: Open-Meteo.com).

5Notifications

Severe weather notifications are off until you turn them on in Settings. Only then does Android ask you for permission to show notifications.

  • When they are on, the app checks your saved places for new alerts in the background, about every 30 or 60 minutes (your choice), using the coordinates already saved on your phone. It never reads your location in the background.
  • Notifications are created on your phone. We do not run a push server and do not receive any token or identifier for your device.
  • You can turn notifications off at any time in the app (Settings > Notifications) or in Android settings.

6Advertising (Google AdMob)

StormPing is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. Ads may also be delivered through AdMob mediation by Unity Ads and Meta Audience Network. We keep ads light, and never let them get in the way of a warning. The app shows:

  • Banner ads at the top of the screen
  • Interstitial ads after you save or choose a place in the Locations screen, at most once every few minutes
  • App open ads when you open the app or return to it after some time
  • Rewarded ads, only if you choose to watch one, to start a 30-minute Ad-Free Break

Never during a warning: full-screen ads (interstitial and app open) are not shown while a Severe or Extreme alert is active at your Home place, on alert screens and the Alerts tab, or after you opened the app from an alert notification.

To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and its mediation partners may automatically collect:

  • Device or other identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location (country or region)
  • App interactions with ads, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostic information, such as ad loading performance and crash logs related to ads

This data is used for advertising, ad measurement, analytics of ad performance, and fraud prevention. It is processed by these companies under their own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Unity Ads: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Meta Audience Network: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

Ads in StormPing are limited to content suitable for teens (Google's "T" rating). The app only sends AdMob ad unit IDs; it never sends your saved locations, GPS position or weather data to any ad network.

7Consent and Your Ad Choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or another region where consent is required, the app shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before loading any ads. You can choose whether to allow personalized ads.

You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings > Ads & privacy > Privacy options.

You can also:

  • Reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization: Android Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads)
  • Learn more about ad personalization: https://adssettings.google.com

If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

8Links to Other Websites and Apps

The app contains a few links that open outside the app: this Privacy Policy, the weather data sources, our developer page on Google Play ("More apps by Craft Horizon"), the app's Google Play page ("Rate StormPing"), Android's share menu ("Share StormPing", which shares a link to the app), and your email app ("Email support"). These open in your browser, Google Play or another app, which have their own privacy policies. The app itself sends no data when you tap them.

9Data Retention and Deletion

  • We do not keep any data about you on our own servers. The app has no servers and no accounts.
  • Data stored on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Cached forecasts and alerts are replaced automatically; notified alert IDs are removed when the alerts expire.
  • The weather services listed in Section 4 may keep ordinary server logs (such as IP address and request time) under their own policies.
  • Advertising data collected by Google and its partners is kept according to their own retention policies. You can reset your Advertising ID at any time in Android settings.

Because the app has no user accounts, there is no account to delete. For any deletion request or question, contact us at the email address in Section 16.

How to request deletion of your data

To request deletion of data related to StormPing: Weather Alerts (developer: Craft Horizon):

  1. Send an email to shopyhoot@gmail.com with the subject "StormPing - Delete my data".
  2. We will answer within 30 days and explain how to delete each kind of data listed below.

What we hold: nothing. The app never sends your locations, settings or other data to us, so we have no records about you to delete.

Data stored on your phone (saved places, last current location, settings, cached weather) can be deleted by you at any time: remove places in the Locations screen, uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings > Apps > StormPing > Storage > Clear data.

Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is controlled by Google. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads, and manage your Google data at https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.

10How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only:

  • With the public weather services in Section 4, as needed to get forecasts and alerts (coordinates of a place, or your search text)
  • With Google (AdMob) and its mediation partners, as described in Section 6, to show and measure ads
  • When required by law, a court order or a government authority, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the app

11Data Security

Your saved places and settings never leave your phone. Requests to the weather services and data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK are encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we use reasonable measures to protect the data.

12Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe and the UK, or under US state privacy laws such as the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict its processing, withdraw your consent, opt out of targeted advertising, and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Because we do not keep personal data about you outside your device, most of these rights can be exercised directly in the app or in your device settings: change your consent in Settings > Ads & privacy > Privacy options, turn off location or notifications, remove saved places, reset your Advertising ID, or uninstall the app. For anything else, contact us at the email address in Section 16. We will answer within 30 days.

13Children's Privacy

StormPing is a general weather app intended for teens aged 13 and over and adults. It is not designed for or directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact us and we will help.

Alerts may be delayed or missing, for example when you are offline or a service is down. In an emergency, always follow instructions from local officials.
