This Privacy Policy explains what information the Cat Grid: Logic Brain Puzzle mobile application ("Cat Grid", "the app", "we", "our") collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android app published on Google Play by CraftHorizon (package: com.crafthorizon.catgrid).

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not install or use the app.

1Summary

  • No account or registration is required.
  • We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or payment information.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google collects device identifiers and related data to show and measure ads.
  • There is no online play, no chat, no leaderboard and no social features. All puzzles are built into the app and played offline.
  • Your game progress (solved levels, stars, best times, daily puzzles and streak, coins, unlocked cats and board frames, settings) is stored only on your phone.
  • The app contains no analytics SDK, no Firebase and no in-app purchases.
  • We do not sell your data.

2Information Stored Only on Your Device

The app saves the following data locally on your phone so the game works between sessions: solved levels with their stars and best times, unfinished puzzles (so you can continue them later), daily puzzle results and your streak, statistics, coins (a cosmetic in-game currency that cannot be bought, wagered or cashed out), unlocked cat styles and board frames, hints, Ad-Free Break time, your settings, whether you have seen the tutorial, and simple counters used to space out ads fairly.

This data is never uploaded to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it permanently. You can also erase all your progress in the app: Settings > About > Reset progress.

3Advertising (Google AdMob)

Cat Grid is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. We keep ads light, and never show them while you are solving a puzzle. The app shows:

  • Banner ads on menu screens (Home, the level map and Statistics), never on the puzzle screen
  • Interstitial ads after a solved puzzle when you tap "Next", at most once every few levels, never during a puzzle and never after the daily puzzle
  • App open ads when you return to the app after some time, never on top of a puzzle
  • Rewarded ads, only if you choose to watch one (for example to get a hint, an extra heart, double coins, or start a 30-minute Ad-Free Break)

To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect:

  • Device or other identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location (country or region)
  • App interactions with ads, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostic information, such as ad loading performance and crash logs related to ads

This data is used for advertising, ad measurement, analytics of ad performance, and fraud prevention. It is processed by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Ads in Cat Grid are limited to content suitable for teens (Google's "T" rating). The app only sends AdMob ad unit IDs; it never sends your game data to any ad network.

4Consent and Your Ad Choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or another region where consent is required, the app shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before loading any ads. You can choose whether to allow personalized ads.

You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings > Privacy options.

You can also:

  • Reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization: Android Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads)
  • Learn more about ad personalization: https://adssettings.google.com

If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

5Links to Other Websites and Apps

The app contains a few links that open outside the app: this Privacy Policy, our developer page on Google Play ("More games by CraftHorizon"), and the app's Google Play page ("Rate Cat Grid"). These open in your browser or the Google Play app, which have their own privacy policies. The app itself sends no data when you tap them.

6Data Retention and Deletion

  • We do not keep any data about you on our own servers. The app has no servers, no accounts and no online features.
  • Data stored on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Advertising data collected by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies. You can reset your Advertising ID at any time in Android settings.

Because the app has no user accounts, there is no account to delete. For any deletion request or question, contact us at the email address in Section 12.

How to request deletion of your data

To request deletion of data related to Cat Grid: Logic Brain Puzzle (developer: CraftHorizon):

  1. Send an email to shopyhoot@gmail.com with the subject "Cat Grid - Delete my data".
  2. We will answer within 30 days and explain how to delete each kind of data listed below.

What we hold: nothing. The app never sends your game data to us, so we have no records about you to delete.

Data stored on your phone (progress, daily puzzles, coins, unlocked cats and board frames, settings) can be deleted by you at any time: in the app (Settings > About > Reset progress), by uninstalling the app, or in Android Settings > Apps > Cat Grid > Storage > Clear data.

Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is controlled by Google. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads, and manage your Google data at https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.

7How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only:

  • With Google (AdMob), as described in Section 3, to show and measure ads
  • When required by law, a court order or a government authority, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the app

8Data Security

Your game data never leaves your phone. Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to Google is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we use reasonable measures to protect the data.

9Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe and the UK, or under US state privacy laws such as the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict its processing, withdraw your consent, opt out of targeted advertising, and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Because we do not keep personal data about you outside your device, most of these rights can be exercised directly in the app or in your device settings: change your consent in Settings > Privacy options, reset your Advertising ID, reset your progress, or uninstall the app. For anything else, contact us at the email address in Section 12. We will answer within 30 days.

10Children's Privacy

Cat Grid is intended for teens aged 13 and over and adults. It is not designed for or directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact us and we will help.
