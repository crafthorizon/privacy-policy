This Privacy Policy explains what information the Pinochle Parlor: Card Game mobile application ("Pinochle Parlor", "the app", "we", "our") collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android app published on Google Play by CraftHorizon (package: com.crafthorizon.pinochle).

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not install or use the app.

1Summary

  • No account or registration is required.
  • We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or payment information.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may also fill ads from its partner networks Unity Ads and Meta Audience Network. These ad services collect device identifiers and related data to show and measure ads.
  • There is no online play, no chat and no multiplayer. You play offline against computer players that run on your phone.
  • Your game progress (game in progress, stats, coins, cosmetics, achievements, settings) is stored only on your phone.
  • The app contains no analytics SDK, no Firebase and no in-app purchases.
  • We do not sell your data.

2Information Stored Only on Your Device

The app saves the following data locally on your phone so the game works between sessions: the game in progress (so you can continue it later), game statistics for each game type, coins (a cosmetic in-game currency that cannot be bought, wagered or cashed out), unlocked card backs and tables, achievements, daily bonus progress, Ad-Free Break time, your settings and house rules, the names you give the computer players, and simple counters used to space out ads fairly.

This data is never uploaded to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it permanently. You can also reset your statistics or all your progress in the app: Settings > Progress.

3Advertising (Google AdMob and Its Ad Partners)

Pinochle Parlor is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. The app shows:

  • Banner ads on menu screens (the Home and Statistics screens), never on the card table
  • Interstitial ads at natural breaks, between some hands or after a game, never during bidding or while a hand is being played
  • App open ads when you return to the app after some time
  • Rewarded ads, only if you choose to watch one (for example to double a daily bonus, earn coins, unlock a card back or table, or start a 30-minute Ad-Free Break)

To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the partner SDKs described below may automatically collect:

  • Device or other identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location (country or region)
  • App interactions with ads, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostic information, such as ad loading performance and crash logs related to ads

This data is used for advertising, ad measurement, analytics of ad performance, and fraud prevention. It is processed by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Ad partners (mediation). To fill more ad requests, AdMob may pass an ad request to one of these partner networks, whose SDKs are included in the app:

  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Meta Audience Network (Meta Platforms): https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy

When a partner shows an ad, its SDK may collect the same kinds of data listed above (device identifiers, IP address and approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics) for the same purposes, under its own privacy policy. The app only sends AdMob ad unit IDs; it never sends your game data to any ad network.

4Consent and Your Ad Choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or another region where consent is required, the app shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before loading any ads. You can choose whether to allow personalized ads. Your choice applies to Google and to its ad partners: Meta Audience Network reads it through AdMob, and the app passes it to Unity Ads directly.

You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings > Ads > Privacy options.

You can also:

  • Reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization: Android Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads)
  • Learn more about ad personalization: https://adssettings.google.com

If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

5Links to Other Websites and Apps

The app contains a few links that open outside the app: this Privacy Policy, our developer page on Google Play ("More games by CraftHorizon"), and the app's Google Play page ("Rate the app"). These open in your browser or the Google Play app, which have their own privacy policies. The app itself sends no data when you tap them.

6Data Retention and Deletion

  • We do not keep any data about you on our own servers. The app has no servers, no accounts and no online features.
  • Data stored on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Advertising data collected by Google and its ad partners (Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network) is kept according to their own retention policies. You can reset your Advertising ID at any time in Android settings.

Because the app has no user accounts, there is no account to delete. For any deletion request or question, contact us at the email address in Section 12.

How to request deletion of your data

To request deletion of data related to Pinochle Parlor: Card Game (developer: CraftHorizon):

  1. Send an email to shopyhoot@gmail.com with the subject "Pinochle Parlor - Delete my data".
  2. We will answer within 30 days and explain how to delete each kind of data listed below.

What we hold: nothing. The app never sends your game data to us, so we have no records about you to delete.

Data stored on your phone (game in progress, stats, coins, cosmetics, achievements and settings) can be deleted by you at any time: in the app (Settings > Progress > Reset all progress), by uninstalling the app, or in Android Settings > Apps > Pinochle Parlor > Storage > Clear data.

Advertising data collected by Google AdMob and its ad partners is controlled by them. You can reset your Advertising ID or delete it in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads, manage your Google data at https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy, and contact Unity or Meta through the privacy policies linked in Section 3.

7How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only:

  • With Google (AdMob) and its ad partners Unity Ads and Meta Audience Network, as described in Section 3, to show and measure ads
  • When required by law, a court order or a government authority, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the app

8Data Security

Your game data never leaves your phone. Data sent by the ads SDKs to Google and its ad partners is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we use reasonable measures to protect the data.

9Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe and the UK, or under US state privacy laws such as the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict its processing, withdraw your consent, opt out of targeted advertising, and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Because we do not keep personal data about you outside your device, most of these rights can be exercised directly in the app or in your device settings: change your consent in Settings > Ads > Privacy options, reset your Advertising ID, reset your progress, or uninstall the app. For anything else, contact us at the email address in Section 12. We will answer within 30 days.

10Children's Privacy

Pinochle Parlor is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not designed for or directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help.
